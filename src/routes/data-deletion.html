Data Deletion Instructions

If you have used Facebook Login to sign in to Ride Bangla and you would like to delete your personal data associated with your account, please follow the steps below.

How to delete your data

1
Open the Ride Bangla app on your device.
2
Go to Account → Settings → Delete Account.
3
Tap "Delete My Account" and confirm. All your personal data, order history, and profile information will be permanently removed within 30 days.
4
Alternatively, you can also revoke Ride Bangla's access directly from your Facebook account: Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites → Ride Bangla → Remove.

Need help?

If you face any issues or want to request data deletion manually, contact us at:
info@ridebangla.bd

Ride Bangla is committed to protecting your privacy. Data deletion requests are processed within 30 days. For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
